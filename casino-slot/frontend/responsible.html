Responsible Play

This page provides responsible gaming guidance. It is not legal advice. If you have concerns about your gaming behavior or finances, seek professional assistance.

Age and Eligibility

You must be of legal age in your jurisdiction (typically 18+) to use this site. Access is prohibited where gambling is illegal.

Set Personal Limits

  • Decide on a budget before you start and stick to it.
  • Consider time limits and take regular breaks.
  • Never chase losses.

Self‑Exclusion and Cool‑Off

If you feel your play is becoming problematic, consider taking a break. Contact support to request a cool‑off or self‑exclusion option if enabled in your region.

Warning Signs

  • Spending more than you can afford or using borrowed money.
  • Neglecting responsibilities due to gambling.
  • Feeling stress, anxiety, or guilt related to play.

Get Help

If you or someone you know needs help, consider these resources:

  • GamCare
  • Gambling Therapy
  • National Council on Problem Gambling (US)
